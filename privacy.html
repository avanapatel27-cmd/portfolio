Privacy policy

Last updated 13 September 2026

This is the personal portfolio website of Dr. Avana Framroz Patel. It exists to show my work. It is not a clinical service and it is not a business. This page explains, plainly, what the site does and does not do with information about you.

What the site collects

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms, no comment boxes, no newsletter and no analytics. I do not use advertising, tracking pixels or third-party cookies, and I do not sell or share visitor data with anyone.

What is stored in your browser

One preference is kept in your browser’s local storage: whether you have muted the ambient sound. It never leaves your device, it contains nothing about you, and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts. The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser requests them, Google receives the technical details of that request, such as your IP address and browser type. Google’s privacy policy applies to that request.
  • jsDelivr. The 3D library used for the DNA helix is loaded from the jsDelivr content delivery network, which receives the same kind of technical request details.
  • Hosting. The site is served by a hosting provider, which may keep standard server logs (IP address, time and page requested) for security and operational reasons for a limited period.

Documents on the site

Research documents and case reports are published as reading copies. They contain no patient-identifiable information. Clinical images and histopathology are withheld and are available from me on request only where appropriate consent exists.

Contact

If you have a question about this page, write to avana.framrozpatel@nhs.net. Emails you send me are handled as ordinary correspondence and are not added to any list.

Changes

If the site ever starts collecting anything, this page will say so first, with the date above updated.
